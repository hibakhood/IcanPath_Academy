## Policy decisions included for approval

1. Late native quiz starts/submissions are denied at or after the deadline. No automatic late passing grade. Existing historical results are retained. Any extension must be explicit, narrow, time bounded and audited; no implicit grace period.
2. Enrollment cancellation immediately denies future assessment operations once cancellation is committed. In flight operations and cancellation are serialized with a consistent lock order; staging tests must validate this.
3. Administrator MFA enforcement is prepared locally and tested with synthetic AAL claims, but production activation follows a successful real enrollment rehearsal and recovery preparation. No unconditional compatibility bypass.
4. Contact form may report temporary unavailability until the trusted server credentials/rate limit source configuration is provided. Public direct database submission is not retained as a fallback.
5. CSP starts report only; full enforcement is a recorded release gate after compatibility testing. No blanket unsafe script allowance.
